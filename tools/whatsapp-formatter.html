WhatsApp Text Formatter

Select text, tap a style, see it exactly as it'll look in the chat — then copy it ready to paste into WhatsApp.

This copies the plain text with WhatsApp's own symbols (*, _, ~, etc.) already in it — paste it straight into a chat and WhatsApp will style it automatically. Select two or more lines before tapping List or Quote to apply it to all of them at once, with numbers counting up correctly.
