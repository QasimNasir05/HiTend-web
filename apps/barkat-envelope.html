What Barkat Envelope does

Barkat Envelope takes the classic envelope-budgeting method — dividing your cash into labelled envelopes for rent, groceries, savings, and so on — and turns it into something you can carry in your pocket. Each envelope has its own animated border that fills up as you spend, so you can see your budget at a glance without opening a single record.

  • Create envelopes with a name, icon or photo, and a spending limit
  • Animated fill border shows how much of each envelope's budget is used
  • Multi-select delete with a trash you can restore from
  • Sort envelopes however makes sense to you
  • Multi-language support, with a custom splash screen and onboarding

Built under HiTend Digital as a fully offline, no-login app — your budgets stay on your device.
