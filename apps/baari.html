What Baari does

Baari organizes a committee (Baari / ROSCA) from setup to payout. Add members, set the round duration, and mark daily payments as they come in — Baari tracks collected and expected totals automatically, and lets the organizer manually confirm who receives each round's payout.

  • Create a committee with members, duration, and per-member photos
  • Daily per-member payment tracking with automatic totals
  • Manual payout confirmation at the end of each round, with a completion screen
  • WhatsApp share for committee and member records
  • Urdu language toggle for the full app interface
  • JSON backup and restore, soft-delete trash for committees

Built under HiTend Digital as an offline, Simple committee (kameti) tracking app for daily payments, member records, and payout tracking. No login needed. Works fully offline. Made for Pakistani committees.
