What Easy Udhar does

Easy Udhar replaces the paper Khata book most small shops still rely on. Add a customer, log what they bought or paid, and the app keeps a running balance for every account — so you always know who owes what, without flipping through pages.

  • Single-shop login with a 4-digit PIN or password
  • Customer records with photos and contact numbers, picked straight from your contacts
  • Automatic balance tracking for sales, purchases, Udhar given, and Udhar paid back
  • Shareable receipts and PDF customer reports, in Urdu
  • Soft-delete trash for customers and records, so nothing is lost by accident
  • Local backup and restore — your data stays on your device

Built and maintained under HiTend Digital, entirely as a native Android app with no backend server involved.
