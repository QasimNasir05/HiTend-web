What PP Editor does

PP Editor walks you through a simple four-step wizard: pick a photo, clean up the background, crop to the exact size your country requires, then export or print. Background removal runs entirely on your device using ML Kit, so nothing leaves your phone.

  • Automatic background removal, with a manual brush for tricky edges
  • Background recoloring to match official requirements (white, blue, red)
  • Size presets for a range of countries' passport and visa photo rules
  • Direct export or print, sized correctly the first time

Built under HiTend Digital as a fast, offline alternative to visiting a photo studio for a single passport-size photo.
