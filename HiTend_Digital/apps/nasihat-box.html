What Nasihat Box does

Nasihat Box reveals one saved piece of wisdom per day, from a library of over 365 preloaded entries — enough for a full year without repeating. There's no scroll, no feed, and nothing pulling you back in more than once a day.

  • One entry unlocked per day, tracked locally on your device
  • 365+ preloaded entries, all available offline
  • Option to add your own entries alongside the preloaded set
  • A short first-time setup that greets you by name

Built under HiTend Digital as a small, quiet app — currently finishing closed testing ahead of a full public release.
